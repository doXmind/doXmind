# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the doXmind community to be welcoming, respectful,
and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and assume good faith.
- Respect different experiences, viewpoints, and communication styles.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@doxmind.com](mailto:support@doxmind.com) with `Code of Conduct` in
the subject line. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and may result in a
warning, content removal, or removal from project spaces.
