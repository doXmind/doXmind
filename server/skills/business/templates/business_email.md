# Business Email Templates

## Request Email

### Template

**Subject**: [Action Type]: [Specific Request] - [Deadline if applicable]

---

Hi [Name],

I hope this message finds you well. [Optional: Brief context if needed]

I'm writing to request [specific request with clear details].

**Background**: [1-2 sentences explaining why this is needed]

**What I Need**:
- [Specific item/action 1]
- [Specific item/action 2]
- [Specific item/action 3]

**Timeline**: I would appreciate having this by [specific date] to [reason for deadline].

Please let me know if you have any questions or need additional information. I'm happy to [offer of assistance/flexibility].

Thank you for your help with this.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

### Example

**Subject**: Request: Q4 Marketing Budget Figures - Needed by March 15

Hi Sarah,

I hope your week is going well.

I'm writing to request the Q4 marketing budget figures for our annual report preparation.

**Background**: We're compiling the annual financial summary for the board presentation scheduled for March 20.

**What I Need**:
- Total Q4 marketing spend by channel
- Comparison to Q4 budget projections
- Any significant variances with brief explanations

**Timeline**: I would appreciate having this by March 15 to allow time for review before the presentation.

Please let me know if you have any questions or need additional context. I'm happy to set up a quick call if that would help.

Thank you for your help with this.

Best regards,
Michael

---

## Follow-Up Email

### Template

**Subject**: Following Up: [Original Topic] - [Action Needed]

---

Hi [Name],

I wanted to follow up on [reference to previous communication/meeting] from [date].

[Brief reminder of what was discussed/requested]

I understand you're busy, but I wanted to check in on the status of [specific item]. [Optional: Explain why timing matters]

**Quick Recap**:
- [Key point 1]
- [Key point 2]

Could you please provide an update by [new date]? If there's anything blocking progress, I'd be happy to help.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

---

### Example

**Subject**: Following Up: Vendor Proposal Review - Decision Needed by Friday

Hi James,

I wanted to follow up on the vendor proposals we discussed in last week's meeting.

The three vendors are awaiting our decision, and two have mentioned their quotes are valid only until month-end.

**Quick Recap**:
- Vendor A: $45,000, 6-week implementation
- Vendor B: $52,000, 4-week implementation
- Vendor C: $38,000, 8-week implementation

Could you please share your recommendation by Friday? If you need additional information to make a decision, I'm happy to arrange calls with any of the vendors.

Thank you,
Lisa

---

## Announcement Email

---

I'm pleased to announce [announcement].

**Key Details**:
- **What**: [Specific change/news]
- **When**: [Effective date/timeline]
- **Impact**: [How this affects the team]

- [Action 2 and owner]

## Apology Email

### Template

**Subject**: Apology: [Brief Description of Issue]

---

Hi [Name],

I want to apologize for [specific issue/mistake].

**What happened**: [Brief, factual explanation without excuses]

**Impact**: I understand this caused [acknowledge the impact on them].

**Corrective action**: [What you've done or will do to fix it]
- [Specific action 1]
- [Specific action 2]

**Prevention**: Going forward, [what you'll do differently to prevent recurrence].

I take full responsibility for this, and I'm committed to ensuring it doesn't happen again. Please let me know if there's anything else I can do to make this right.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

---

### Example

**Subject**: Apology: Incorrect Data in Yesterday's Report

Hi Catherine,

I want to apologize for the incorrect revenue figures in yesterday's quarterly report.

**What happened**: I used data from an outdated spreadsheet, which resulted in Q3 numbers being understated by 15%.

**Impact**: I understand this caused confusion in the executive meeting and required you to issue a correction.

**Corrective action**:
- I've sent a corrected report to all recipients
- I've archived the outdated file to prevent future use

**Prevention**: Going forward, I'll implement a verification step to confirm I'm using the most current data source before finalizing any reports.

I take full responsibility for this error, and I'm committed to ensuring accuracy in all future reports. Please let me know if there's anything else I can do.

Sincerely,
David

## Declining a Request Email

### Template

**Subject**: Re: [Original Request]

---

Hi [Name],

Thank you for thinking of me for [request/opportunity].

After careful consideration, I'm not able to [accept/participate/commit] at this time due to [brief, honest reason].

[Optional: Alternative or partial solution you can offer]

I appreciate you reaching out, and I hope we can [future opportunity/collaboration].

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## Introduction Email

### Template

**Subject**: Introduction: [Your Name] - [Context/Role]

---

Hi [Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I'm [your role/relationship to recipient].

[How you got their contact/who referred you]

I'm reaching out because [specific reason/what you're hoping to accomplish].

**Brief Background**:
[2-3 sentences about relevant experience or context]

I would appreciate [specific ask - a meeting, advice, connection, etc.]. [Make it easy for them to help - suggest specific times, offer flexibility]

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Contact Information]

| Question | Question: [Topic] | Question: Q3 Launch Timeline |
| FYI | FYI: [Topic] | FYI: New Office Hours |
| Urgent | Urgent: [Issue] - [Action] | Urgent: Server Down - Help Needed |
| Meeting | Meeting: [Topic] - [Date] | Meeting: Sprint Planning - Jan 15 |
| Follow-up | Following Up: [Topic] | Following Up: Proposal Review |
