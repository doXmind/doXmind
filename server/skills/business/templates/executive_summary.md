## Writing Guidelines for Executive Summaries

### Length
- Typically 1-2 pages maximum
- Should take 3-5 minutes to read
- Can be read independently from full report

### Structure
- Lead with the most important information
- Use bullet points and tables for quick scanning
- Include only essential data points
- Make recommendations actionable

### Tone
- Confident but not arrogant
- Data-driven, not opinion-driven
- Clear about uncertainties
- Action-oriented

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Including too much detail
- Burying the recommendation
- Using jargon without explanation
- Failing to specify what decision is needed
- Not providing a clear recommendation
