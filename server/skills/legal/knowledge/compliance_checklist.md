# Compliance Checklist

Key regulatory frameworks and compliance requirements for legal documents.

## Privacy Regulations

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

**Applies to**: Organizations processing personal data of EU residents

#### Key Requirements

**Lawful Basis for Processing**
- [ ] Identified lawful basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, legitimate interests)
- [ ] Documented the lawful basis
- [ ] If consent-based, consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous
- [ ] Consent can be withdrawn as easily as given

**Transparency**
- [ ] Privacy notice provided at data collection
- [ ] Notice includes: identity, contact details, purposes, legal basis, recipients, retention period, rights
- [ ] Notice in clear, plain language
- [ ] Separate consent for different purposes

**Data Subject Rights**
- [ ] Process for access requests (30 days)
- [ ] Process for rectification
- [ ] Process for erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- [ ] Process for restriction of processing
- [ ] Process for data portability
- [ ] Process for objection to processing
- [ ] Process for automated decision-making/profiling objections

**Data Protection Principles**
- [ ] Purpose limitation (specific, explicit, legitimate purposes)
- [ ] Data minimization (adequate, relevant, limited)
- [ ] Accuracy (kept up to date)
- [ ] Storage limitation (kept only as long as necessary)
- [ ] Integrity and confidentiality (security measures)
- [ ] Accountability (documented compliance)

**International Transfers**
- [ ] Identified all international data transfers
- [ ] Adequate transfer mechanism (adequacy decision, SCCs, BCRs, consent)
- [ ] Transfer impact assessment for high-risk transfers

**Security and Breach**
- [ ] Appropriate technical and organizational measures
- [ ] Data breach notification procedure (72 hours to authority)
- [ ] Data breach notification to affected individuals (high risk)

**Documentation**
- [ ] Records of processing activities
- [ ] Data Protection Impact Assessment (high-risk processing)
- [ ] Data Protection Officer appointed (if required)

### CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act)

**Applies to**: Businesses meeting thresholds that collect California residents' data

#### Key Requirements

**Consumer Rights**
- [ ] Right to know (what data collected, sources, purposes, third parties)
- [ ] Right to delete
- [ ] Right to opt-out of sale/sharing
- [ ] Right to correct inaccurate information
- [ ] Right to limit use of sensitive personal information
- [ ] Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

**Notice Requirements**
- [ ] Privacy policy disclosed
- [ ] "At collection" notice provided
- [ ] Notice of right to opt-out
- [ ] Notice of financial incentive programs (if any)

**Operational Requirements**
- [ ] Two methods for submitting requests (one being toll-free number for businesses with offline operations)
- [ ] Verification procedures for requests
- [ ] Response within 45 days (extendable by 45 more)
- [ ] "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link (if selling/sharing)
- [ ] "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link (if applicable)
- [ ] Service provider/contractor agreements with required provisions

**Documentation**
- [ ] Records of requests and responses (24 months)
- [ ] Training for personnel handling requests

## Website and App Compliance

### Cookie Compliance

**EU (ePrivacy Directive + GDPR)**
- [ ] Cookie banner/notice displayed
- [ ] Consent obtained before non-essential cookies
- [ ] Easy to reject as to accept
- [ ] Granular consent options (by category)
- [ ] Cookie policy detailing all cookies
- [ ] Consent records maintained
- [ ] Easy withdrawal of consent

**CCPA/CPRA**
- [ ] Cookie disclosure in privacy policy
- [ ] Opt-out of "sale" via cookies (if applicable)

### Accessibility

**WCAG 2.1 (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)**
- [ ] Level A compliance (minimum)
- [ ] Level AA compliance (recommended)
- [ ] Perceivable (text alternatives, captions, contrast)
- [ ] Operable (keyboard accessible, enough time, no seizures)
- [ ] Understandable (readable, predictable, input assistance)
- [ ] Robust (compatible with assistive technology)

**ADA Compliance (US)**
- [ ] Website accessible to people with disabilities
- [ ] Accessibility statement published
- [ ] Feedback mechanism for accessibility issues

### Children's Privacy

**COPPA (US - Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)**
- [ ] Determined if service is directed to children under 13
- [ ] Determined if knowingly collecting from children under 13
- [ ] Verifiable parental consent before collection
- [ ] Direct notice to parents
- [ ] Privacy policy addresses children's data
- [ ] Parental access and deletion rights

**GDPR (Children)**
- [ ] Parental consent for children under 16 (or lower age set by member state)
- [ ] Age verification mechanism
- [ ] Privacy notice understandable by children

## Industry-Specific Regulations

### Financial Services

**PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)**
- [ ] Build and maintain secure network
- [ ] Protect cardholder data
- [ ] Vulnerability management program
- [ ] Strong access control measures
- [ ] Regular monitoring and testing
- [ ] Information security policy

**SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley)**
- [ ] Internal controls over financial reporting
- [ ] Management certification
- [ ] Auditor attestation (accelerated filers)
- [ ] Whistleblower protections

### Healthcare

**HIPAA (US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)**
- [ ] Notice of Privacy Practices
- [ ] Patient authorization for non-routine disclosures
- [ ] Minimum necessary standard
- [ ] Business Associate Agreements
- [ ] Administrative safeguards
- [ ] Physical safeguards
- [ ] Technical safeguards
- [ ] Breach notification (60 days)

### Employment

**Workplace Privacy**
- [ ] Employee monitoring disclosed
- [ ] Background check compliance (FCRA)
- [ ] Biometric data consent (state laws)
- [ ] Employee data privacy notices

## Contract Compliance

### Terms of Service Requirements

**General**
- [ ] Clear acceptance mechanism
- [ ] Modification procedure with notice
- [ ] Termination provisions
- [ ] Liability limitations (reasonable)
- [ ] Dispute resolution clause
- [ ] Governing law specified

**Consumer Protection**
- [ ] No unfair terms (EU Unfair Contract Terms Directive)
- [ ] Conspicuous disclaimers
- [ ] Clear cancellation rights
- [ ] Refund policies compliant with local law

**Digital Content (EU)**
- [ ] Conformity guarantees
- [ ] Update obligations disclosed
- [ ] Remedy rights (repair, replace, refund)

### Privacy Policy Requirements

**Universal Elements**
- [ ] Identity and contact information
- [ ] Types of data collected
- [ ] Purposes of processing
- [ ] Third-party sharing
- [ ] Retention periods
- [ ] Security measures
- [ ] User rights
- [ ] How to exercise rights
- [ ] Last updated date

**Jurisdiction-Specific**
- [ ] GDPR: Lawful basis, international transfers, DPO contact
- [ ] CCPA: Categories of data, "sale" disclosure, opt-out instructions
- [ ] Other: Local requirements addressed

## Marketing and Communications

### Email Marketing

**CAN-SPAM (US)**
- [ ] No false or misleading headers
- [ ] No deceptive subject lines
- [ ] Identified as advertisement (if applicable)
- [ ] Physical postal address included
- [ ] Opt-out mechanism (10 business days to honor)
- [ ] Honor opt-outs promptly

**GDPR/ePrivacy**
- [ ] Consent for marketing (B2C)
- [ ] Soft opt-in for existing customers (limited)
- [ ] Easy unsubscribe
- [ ] Records of consent

**CASL (Canada)**
- [ ] Express or implied consent
- [ ] Sender identification
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism
- [ ] Honor unsubscribe (10 business days)

### Telemarketing

**TCPA (US)**
- [ ] Prior express written consent for autodialed/prerecorded calls
- [ ] Do Not Call list compliance
- [ ] Caller ID requirements
- [ ] Time restrictions (8am-9pm local)

## AI and Automated Systems

### AI Transparency

**EU AI Act (upcoming)**
- [ ] Risk classification of AI system
- [ ] High-risk: conformity assessment, registration
- [ ] Transparency obligations for certain AI
- [ ] Human oversight requirements
- [ ] Record-keeping

**General Best Practices**
- [ ] Disclose AI-generated content
- [ ] Explain automated decision-making
- [ ] Provide human review option
- [ ] Address bias and fairness
- [ ] Document training data and methodology

- [ ] Identify all jurisdictions where users are located

- [ ] Local legal counsel for major markets
- [ ] Language requirements met

- [ ] Tax and invoicing requirements

## Quick Compliance Check

- [ ] Privacy policy live and linked
- [ ] Terms of service live and linked
- [ ] Cookie banner functional (if required)
- [ ] Contact information accurate
- [ ] Data subject request process tested
- [ ] Opt-out mechanisms functional
- [ ] Third-party services reviewed

- [ ] Monitor regulatory changes
- [ ] Update policies as needed
- [ ] Respond to data subject requests timely
- [ ] Report breaches as required
- [ ] Train new employees
- [ ] Audit third parties
- [ ] Document everything
