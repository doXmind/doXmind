## Numbers & Specificity

Specific numbers increase credibility.

### Effective Patterns
- "127% increase" (specific > "more than double")
- "10,847 customers" (specific > "thousands")
- "In just 7 minutes" (specific > "quickly")
- "3 simple steps" (specific > "easy process")

### Power Numbers
- 3, 5, 7 (easily digestible lists)
- 10, 100 (round, impactful)
- Odd numbers (feel more authentic)
- Specific decimals (feel researched)

## Words to Avoid

### Weak Words
- Maybe
- Might
- Could
- Possibly
- Somewhat
- Kind of
- Sort of
- Hopefully

### Overused/Spam Triggers
- Revolutionary (overused)
- Act now!!! (excessive punctuation)
- 100% guaranteed (often spam-filtered)
- No obligation (sounds like a catch)
- Once in a lifetime (unbelievable)

### Vague Words
- Nice
- Good
- Great
- Stuff
- Things
- Various
- Different
- Many
