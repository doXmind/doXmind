# Copywriting Formulas

Proven frameworks for persuasive writing that converts.

---

## AIDA

The classic foundation of persuasive copy.

### Structure

**A - Attention**
Grab attention with a compelling headline or opening.
- Bold claim
- Surprising fact
- Provocative question
- Relatable problem

**I - Interest**
Build interest by connecting to their situation.
- Expand on the hook
- Show you understand their problem
- Present relevant information
- Create "yes, that's me" moments

**D - Desire**
Create desire for your solution.
- Paint the after picture
- Show benefits (not features)
- Use social proof
- Help them visualize success

**A - Action**
Tell them exactly what to do.
- Clear CTA
- Remove friction
- Add urgency if appropriate
- Make it easy

### Example

**Attention**: "Spending 10+ hours a week on social media with nothing to show for it?"

**Interest**: "You're not alone. 73% of marketers feel the same way. The problem isn't you—it's that you're doing it manually."

**Desire**: "Imagine scheduling a whole month of content in one hour, while your engagement doubles. Our customers see an average 3x increase in followers within 90 days."

**Action**: "Start your free trial today. No credit card required."

## PAS

The psychology of pain sells.

### Structure

**P - Problem**
Identify the problem they're facing.
- Be specific
- Use their language
- Show you understand

**A - Agitate**
Make the problem feel urgent.
- What's it costing them?
- What happens if it continues?
- Emotional impact

**S - Solution**
Present your solution as the answer.
- Position as relief
- Show it solves the exact problem
- Make it feel inevitable

### Example

**Problem**: "Your email open rates are stuck at 15%."

**Agitate**: "That means 85% of your list never sees your content. Every email you send, you're leaving money on the table. And the longer this continues, the more your list goes cold."

**Solution**: "Our AI subject line optimizer has helped 500+ businesses boost open rates by an average of 47%. Plug it in, and watch your emails get read."

---

## PASO

PAS with an added outcome.

### Structure

**P - Problem**: Identify the pain
**A - Agitate**: Amplify urgency
**S - Solution**: Present your answer
**O - Outcome**: Paint the result

### When to Use

Long-form copy where you want to paint a vivid "after" picture.

---

## FAB

Connect features to real benefits.

### Structure

**F - Feature**: What it is/does
**A - Advantage**: Why that matters
**B - Benefit**: How it improves their life

### Example

| Feature | Advantage | Benefit |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| "AI-powered writing" | "Creates content in seconds" | "Get 10 hours back every week" |
| "24/7 support" | "Help when you need it" | "Never feel stuck or alone" |
| "One-click export" | "Works with any platform" | "No tech hassle, just results" |

### Pro Tip

Always end on the benefit. Features tell, benefits sell.

## BAB

Before-After-Bridge—transformation storytelling.

### Structure

**B - Before**
Paint the current painful state.
- What's frustrating them
- What's not working
- How they feel

**A - After**
Paint the desired future state.
- What life looks like with solution
- How they'll feel
- What they'll achieve

**B - Bridge**
Show how your product connects them.
- This is where you introduce your offer
- Position as the path from before to after

### Example

**Before**: "You're drowning in busywork. Every day feels like a scramble just to keep up. By Friday, you're exhausted and behind."

**After**: "Imagine ending each day with your to-do list cleared. Having time for strategic thinking. Feeling in control instead of overwhelmed."

**Bridge**: "[Product] automates the repetitive tasks eating your time. Join 10,000 professionals who've reclaimed their workday."

## 4Ps

Promise-driven framework.

### Structure

**P - Promise**
What will you deliver? Make a bold, specific claim.

**P - Picture**
Help them visualize the outcome. Use sensory language.

**P - Proof**
Provide evidence. Testimonials, data, case studies.

**P - Push**
Drive action. CTA with urgency.

### Example

**Promise**: "Double your website traffic in 90 days."

**Picture**: "Imagine checking your analytics and seeing 10,000 visitors instead of 5,000. More leads, more sales, more growth."

**Proof**: "We've done this for 500+ businesses. Average traffic increase: 127%. Here's what Sarah said: 'From 3,000 to 15,000 visitors in 3 months.'"

**Push**: "Get your free traffic audit today. Limited to 10 businesses this week."

## PASTOR

Comprehensive framework for longer copy.

### Structure

**P - Problem**
Identify and describe the problem.

**A - Amplify**
Make consequences clear. What's at stake?

**S - Story/Solution**
Tell a relatable story, then introduce your solution.

**T - Transformation**
Show the before and after. What change happens?

**O - Offer**
Present exactly what they get.

**R - Response**
Clear call-to-action.

### Best For

Sales pages, long-form landing pages, webinar pitches.

---

## The 4 Cs

Content quality framework.

### Structure

**Clear**
- No jargon
- Simple language
- One idea at a time

**Concise**
- Cut unnecessary words
- Get to the point
- Respect their time

**Compelling**
- Benefits-focused
- Emotionally resonant
- Creates desire

**Credible**
- Supported by proof
- Specific claims
- Trustworthy voice

## The 4 Us (Headlines)

### Structure

**Useful** - Promises value to the reader
**Urgent** - Creates time pressure
**Unique** - Different from competitors
**Ultra-specific** - Concrete and precise

### Scoring Headlines

Rate each headline 1-4 on each U. Aim for 12+.

---

## Star-Chain-Hook

Story-based persuasion.

### Structure

**Star**: Introduce the main character (often the reader or someone like them)

**Chain**: Build a chain of facts, benefits, and reasons connected to their problem

**Hook**: End with a compelling offer or call-to-action

### Best For

Email marketing, narrative-based copy.

---

## ACCA

Awareness-based framework.

### Structure

**A - Awareness**: Make them aware of the problem
**C - Comprehension**: Help them understand it
**C - Conviction**: Convince them of your solution
**A - Action**: Drive the action

### When to Use

When your audience may not fully recognize the problem yet.

---

## Formula Selection Guide

| Situation | Best Formula |
|-----------|-------------|
| Quick sales page | PAS |
| Long-form sales | PASTOR |
| Email copy | BAB or PAS |
| Headlines | 4Us |
| Product descriptions | FAB |
| General structure | AIDA |
| Storytelling | Star-Chain-Hook |
| Feature marketing | FAB |
| Transformation focus | BAB |

---

## Combining Formulas

Most great copy combines elements:

**Sales Page Example**:
1. AIDA for overall structure
2. PAS for problem section
3. FAB for features section
4. 4Ps for closing section

**Email Example**:
1. Hook (Attention from AIDA)
2. BAB for body
3. CTA (Action from AIDA)
