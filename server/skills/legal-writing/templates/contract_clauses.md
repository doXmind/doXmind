# Contract Clause Library

Common contract clauses with plain language explanations.

**Note**: Adapt to your specific situation. Have an attorney review before use.

---

## Definitions

### Purpose
Define key terms to ensure consistent interpretation throughout the agreement.

### Template

```
1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"Agreement" means this [Agreement Type], including all exhibits and schedules.

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, including but not limited to [business plans, customer lists, technical data, etc.].

"Effective Date" means [date] or the date both parties sign this Agreement, whichever is later.

"Services" means [description of services].

"Term" means the period from the Effective Date until [end date or termination].
```

### Purpose
Clearly define what services will be provided.

### Template

```

2.1 Provider agrees to perform the following services ("Services"):
   (a) [Service 1]
   (b) [Service 2]

## Payment Terms

### Purpose
Establish payment obligations, timing, and methods.

### Template

```
3. PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 Fees. Client agrees to pay Provider:
   (a) [Fee structure - flat fee, hourly, milestone-based]
   (b) Total amount: [Amount]

3.2 Payment Schedule.
   (a) [Amount] due upon signing
   (b) [Amount] due upon [milestone]
   (c) [Amount] due upon completion

3.3 Payment Method. Payments shall be made by [method] within [X] days of invoice date.

3.4 Late Payments. Late payments accrue interest at [X]% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

3.5 Expenses. [Client agrees to reimburse reasonable expenses / All expenses are included in fees].

## Confidentiality

### Purpose
Protect sensitive information shared between parties.

### Template

```
4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient") that is:
   (a) Marked as confidential; or
   (b) Should reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature.

4.2 Obligations. Recipient agrees to:
   (a) Use Confidential Information only to fulfill obligations under this Agreement
   (b) Protect Confidential Information with at least the same care used for its own confidential information
   (c) Limit access to employees and agents who need to know
   (d) Not disclose to third parties without prior written consent

4.3 Exceptions. Confidential Information does not include information that:
   (a) Is or becomes publicly available without breach
   (b) Was known to Recipient before disclosure
   (c) Is received from a third party without restriction
   (d) Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
   (e) Must be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to Discloser)

4.4 Duration. This confidentiality obligation survives for [X] years after termination.
```

## Intellectual Property

### Purpose
Establish ownership of work product and pre-existing IP.

### Template

```
5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Pre-Existing IP. Each party retains ownership of intellectual property it owned before this Agreement.

5.2 Work Product. [Choose one option:]

   Option A - Client Ownership:
   All work product created under this Agreement ("Work Product") is "work made for hire" and belongs to Client. Provider assigns all rights in Work Product to Client.

   Option B - Provider Ownership with License:
   Provider retains ownership of Work Product. Provider grants Client a [non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free] license to use Work Product for [Client's business purposes].

   Option C - Joint Ownership:
   Work Product is jointly owned. Each party may use it without the other's consent.

5.3 Provider Materials. Provider retains ownership of pre-existing tools, methods, and know-how used in creating Work Product.
```

## Representations and Warranties

### Purpose
Each party makes certain promises about their authority and performance.

### Template

```
6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

6.1 Mutual Representations. Each party represents and warrants that:
   (a) It has the authority to enter into this Agreement
   (b) This Agreement does not conflict with other obligations
   (c) It will comply with all applicable laws

6.2 Provider Warranties. Provider represents and warrants that:
   (a) Services will be performed in a professional manner
   (b) Work Product will be original and not infringe third-party rights
   (c) Provider has the skills and qualifications to perform Services

6.3 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH HEREIN, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
```

## Limitation of Liability

### Purpose
Cap potential damages to manage risk.

### Template

```
7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 Exclusion of Damages. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, DATA, OR BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES.

7.2 Cap on Liability. EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED [THE GREATER OF $[AMOUNT] OR THE FEES PAID/PAYABLE IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM].

7.3 Exceptions. These limitations do not apply to:
   (a) Breaches of confidentiality
   (b) Infringement of intellectual property rights
   (c) Gross negligence or willful misconduct
   (d) Payment obligations
```

## Indemnification

### Purpose
Allocate responsibility for third-party claims.

### Template

```
8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Provider Indemnity. Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Client from claims arising from:
   (a) Provider's breach of this Agreement
   (b) Provider's negligence or misconduct
   (c) Infringement claims related to Work Product

8.2 Client Indemnity. Client shall indemnify and hold harmless Provider from claims arising from:
   (a) Client's breach of this Agreement
   (b) Client's use of Services beyond the scope of this Agreement
   (c) Client-provided materials

8.3 Procedure. The indemnified party shall:
   (a) Promptly notify the indemnifying party
   (b) Allow the indemnifying party to control the defense
   (c) Cooperate in the defense
```

## Termination

### Purpose
Establish how and when the agreement can end.

### Template

```
9. TERMINATION

9.1 Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until [fixed date / completion of Services / termination].

9.2 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate with [X] days' written notice.

9.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party:
   (a) Materially breaches and fails to cure within [X] days of notice
   (b) Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

9.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:
   (a) All outstanding payments become due
   (b) Each party returns or destroys the other's Confidential Information
   (c) Provisions that should survive termination will survive [Sections X, X, X]
```

## Force Majeure

### Purpose
Excuse non-performance due to events beyond control.

### Template

```
10. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party is liable for failure or delay caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, terrorism, pandemics, government actions, or infrastructure failures ("Force Majeure Event").

The affected party must:
(a) Promptly notify the other party
(b) Use reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact
(c) Resume performance as soon as possible

If Force Majeure continues for more than [X] days, either party may terminate without liability.
```

## Dispute Resolution

### Purpose
Establish process for resolving disagreements.

### Template

```
11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 Negotiation. The parties will first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation.

11.2 Mediation. If negotiation fails, disputes will be submitted to mediation before [organization] in [location].

11.3 [Arbitration/Litigation]. If mediation fails:

   Option A - Arbitration:
   Disputes will be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of [arbitration organization] in [location]. The arbitrator's decision is final and binding.

   Option B - Litigation:
   Either party may pursue legal action in the courts of [jurisdiction].

11.4 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country].
```

## General Provisions

### Template

```
12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.

12.2 Amendment. This Agreement may only be modified in writing signed by both parties.

12.3 Waiver. Failure to enforce any provision does not waive that right.

12.4 Severability. If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

12.5 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without written consent, except to an affiliate or successor.

12.6 Notices. All notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses below by [email/mail/both].

12.7 Independent Contractor. Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee.

12.8 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original.
```
