# Argumentative Essay Template

## Title
[Your Essay Title]

## Introduction

[Hook - Start with an engaging opening: a surprising fact, question, quote, or brief anecdote related to your topic]

[Context - Provide 2-3 sentences of background information that helps the reader understand the topic and its importance]

[Thesis Statement - State your main argument clearly and specifically. This should be debatable, not a fact.]

**Example thesis structure:** "Although [opposing viewpoint], [your position] because [reason 1], [reason 2], and [reason 3]."

---

## Body Paragraph 1: Strongest Argument

**Topic Sentence:** [State the first reason supporting your thesis]

**Evidence:** [Present facts, statistics, expert opinions, or examples that support this point]

**Explanation:** [Analyze how this evidence supports your argument. Don't assume it's obvious - explain the connection]

**Transition:** [Connect to the next paragraph]

---

## Body Paragraph 2: Second Argument

**Topic Sentence:** [State the second reason supporting your thesis]

**Evidence:** [Present supporting evidence]

**Explanation:** [Analyze the evidence and its significance]

**Transition:** [Connect to the next paragraph]

---

## Body Paragraph 3: Third Argument or Counterargument

**Topic Sentence:** [State your third reason OR address the strongest opposing argument]

**If presenting third argument:**
- Evidence and explanation as above

**If addressing counterargument:**
- Present the opposing view fairly
- Explain why your position is stronger
- Use evidence to refute the counterargument

**Transition:** [Signal you're moving to the conclusion]

---

## Conclusion

[Restate thesis - Paraphrase your thesis in different words]

[Summarize main points - Briefly touch on your key arguments without repeating them verbatim]

[Broader implications - Explain why this argument matters. What are the consequences? What should be done?]

[Closing statement - End with a thought-provoking sentence, call to action, or connection to a larger issue]
