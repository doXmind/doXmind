## Headline

**[Organization] [Action Verb] [What]: [Key Detail or Benefit]**

Guidelines:
- Be specific and newsworthy
- Include organization name
- Use present tense or action verbs
- Keep to one line (65-80 characters ideal)
- Avoid jargon and hype

**Examples**:
- "Acme Corp Launches AI-Powered Customer Service Platform"
- "City Hospital Opens New Pediatric Care Center"
- "TechStart Raises $50 Million in Series B Funding"
- "Local Nonprofit Expands Free Tutoring to Five New Schools"

### Subheadline (Optional)

*[Secondary detail that adds context or key benefit—one line]*

**Example**:
*New facility will serve 5,000 children annually in underserved communities*

---

## Dateline and Lead Paragraph

**[CITY, State]** — [Date] — [The lead paragraph should answer WHO is doing WHAT, WHEN, and WHY it matters. Include the most newsworthy information. Keep to 2-3 sentences.]

**Formula**: [Organization] [action] [what] [when/where]. [Why it matters or key benefit]. [Important detail.]

**Example**:
"**SAN FRANCISCO, Calif.** — January 15, 2024 — TechStart Inc. today announced it has raised $50 million in Series B funding led by Innovation Ventures. The investment will accelerate the company's expansion into European and Asian markets while doubling its engineering team. TechStart's platform has grown 300% in the past year, now serving over 10,000 businesses."

---

## Second Paragraph: Key Details

[Expand on the announcement with important supporting details]

Include:
- Specific features, benefits, or scope
- Numbers and data points
- Timeline or next steps
- What makes this significant

**Example**:
"The new pediatric center features 50 private patient rooms, a dedicated emergency department, and specialized units for oncology, cardiology, and neurology. Construction began in 2022, and the facility incorporates the latest in child-friendly design, including interactive art installations and natural light throughout."

---

## Quote from Leadership

[Include a quote from a senior executive or spokesperson that adds perspective, vision, or emotional element]

**Formula**: "Statement about significance, vision, or impact," said [Full Name], [full title] of [Organization]. "Additional statement about future or context."

**Example**:
"This funding validates our mission to make enterprise software accessible to businesses of all sizes," said Maria Chen, CEO and co-founder of TechStart. "We're excited to bring our platform to new markets and continue innovating for our customers."

**Quote Tips**:
- Make it quotable—something a journalist would actually use
- Avoid jargon and corporate speak
- Include something only this person could say
- Keep each quote to 2-3 sentences

## Press Release Formatting Rules

### Format
- Single-spaced
- Standard margins (1 inch)
- Professional font (Times New Roman, Arial, 11-12pt)
- Organization letterhead
- One to two pages maximum

### Style
- Write in third person
- Use past tense for completed actions, present for ongoing
- Include dateline with city and date
- End with ### or — END —

### Numbers
- Spell out one through nine
- Use numerals for 10 and above
- Always use numerals for money, percentages, ages
- Include % symbol (not "percent") for readability

### What to Avoid
- Excessive adjectives ("revolutionary," "groundbreaking")
- Unsubstantiated claims
- Insider jargon
- Lengthy paragraphs
- More than two pages
