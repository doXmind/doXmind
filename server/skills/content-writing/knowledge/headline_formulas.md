# Headline Formulas & Techniques

## The Science of Headlines

Headlines determine whether your content gets read. Studies show:
- 80% of people read headlines, only 20% read the full content
- Headlines with numbers get 36% more clicks
- Negative headlines ("mistakes to avoid") can outperform positive ones by 30%
- Odd numbers outperform even numbers in listicles

---

## Core Headline Formulas

### 1. How-To Headlines

**Formula**: How to [Achieve Desired Outcome] [Timeframe/Qualifier]

**Examples:**
- How to Write a Blog Post That Ranks on Google
- How to Double Your Email Open Rates in 30 Days
- How to Build a Landing Page Without Code

**Variations:**
- How [Audience] Can [Achieve Goal]
- How I [Achieved Result] (And You Can Too)
- The Simple Way to [Achieve Outcome]

---

### 2. List Headlines (Listicles)

**Formula**: [Number] [Noun] to [Achieve Benefit/Avoid Problem]

**Examples:**
- 7 Email Marketing Mistakes That Kill Your Open Rates
- 15 Tools Every Content Writer Needs in 2024
- 5 Habits of Highly Productive Writers

**Number Psychology:**
| Number | Best For |
|--------|----------|
| 3-5 | Quick tips, focused guides |
| 7-10 | Comprehensive lists |
| 10+ | Ultimate guides, "everything you need" |
| Odd numbers | Higher engagement (5, 7, 9, 11) |
| Specific numbers | More credible (37 vs 40) |

---

### 3. Question Headlines

**Formula**: [Question that addresses reader's pain/desire]?

**Examples:**
- Why Isn't Your Content Ranking on Google?
- What's Killing Your Conversion Rate?
- Are You Making These Common Writing Mistakes?
- Want to Double Your Traffic? Here's How

**Best Practices:**
- Question should resonate with target audience's real concern
- Reader should mentally answer "yes" or "I want to know"
- Follow with content that delivers the answer

---

### 4. Curiosity Gap Headlines

**Formula**: [Intriguing statement that creates information gap]

**Examples:**
- The One Thing Most Writers Get Wrong About Headlines
- What Nobody Tells You About Building an Audience
- The Counterintuitive Secret to Writing Faster
- I Tried [Method] for 30 Days. Here's What Happened

**Keys to Curiosity Gap:**
- Hint at valuable information without revealing it
- Create a knowledge gap the reader wants to fill
- Always deliver on the promise in the content

---

### 5. Negative/Warning Headlines

**Formula**: [Number] [Mistakes/Errors/Things] That [Negative Outcome]

**Examples:**
- 5 SEO Mistakes That Are Destroying Your Rankings
- Stop Doing These 3 Things With Your Email List
- Why Your Blog Posts Are Failing (And How to Fix It)
- The Hidden Dangers of [Common Practice]

**Why They Work:**
- Loss aversion: People fear losing more than they desire gaining
- Curiosity: "Am I making these mistakes?"
- Problem-aware audiences respond strongly

---

### 6. "Ultimate Guide" Headlines

**Formula**: The [Ultimate/Complete/Definitive] Guide to [Topic]

**Examples:**
- The Complete Guide to Content Marketing in 2024
- The Ultimate SEO Checklist for Beginners
- Everything You Need to Know About Email Automation

**When to Use:**
- Long-form, comprehensive content (3000+ words)
- Pillar pages targeting competitive keywords
- When you're genuinely covering the topic exhaustively

---

### 7. Comparison Headlines

**Formula**: [Option A] vs [Option B]: [Decision Factor]

**Examples:**
- Mailchimp vs ConvertKit: Which Is Best for Beginners?
- Short-Form vs Long-Form Content: What Works Better?
- Morning vs Evening: The Best Time to Post on LinkedIn

---

### 8. Benefit-Driven Headlines

**Formula**: [Action] to [Specific Benefit] in [Timeframe]

**Examples:**
- Write Blog Posts 3x Faster With This Simple Method
- Get More Email Subscribers Without Paid Ads
- Turn Website Visitors Into Customers Using These 5 Strategies

---

## Power Words by Category

### Urgency
- Now, Today, Immediately, Quick, Fast, Instant, Limited

### Exclusivity
- Secret, Insider, Hidden, Little-known, Exclusive, Underground

### Authority
- Proven, Expert, Science-backed, Research-based, Data-driven

### Value
- Free, Essential, Ultimate, Complete, Comprehensive, Revolutionary

### Emotion
- Surprising, Shocking, Incredible, Inspiring, Heartbreaking

### Action
- Boost, Skyrocket, Transform, Unlock, Discover, Master, Crush

## 中文标题技巧 (Chinese Headline Techniques)

### 小红书标题公式

**公式1: 数字 + 名词 + 效果**
- 5个习惯让我一个月瘦了10斤
- 3招搞定职场沟通，亲测有效
- 7天学会Python，零基础入门指南

**公式2: 人群 + 必看/必读 + 主题**
- 新手必看！护肤入门全攻略
- 打工人必读的时间管理技巧
- 留学生必备的租房避坑指南

**公式3: 情绪词 + 结果**
- 后悔没早点知道的xx技巧
- 惊！这样做竟然可以xx
- 绝了！xx的正确打开方式

**公式4: 对比/反差**
- 以前 vs 现在：我的xx变化
- 月薪3千和月薪3万的区别
- 同样是xx，为什么效果差这么多

### 微信公众号标题技巧

**类型1: 悬念型**
- 为什么越努力的人越容易失败？
- 那些年薪百万的人，都做对了什么？

**类型2: 痛点型**
- 90%的人都不知道的xx真相
- 你以为的xx，其实是错的

**类型3: 利益型**
- 学会这3点，让你的收入翻倍
- 掌握这个技能，少走10年弯路

**类型4: 热点借势**
- 从xx事件看xx的重要性
- xx爆火背后的xx逻辑

## Headline Testing

### A/B Testing Elements

Test one variable at a time:
- Number vs no number
- Question vs statement
- Positive vs negative framing
- Specific vs general benefit
- Short vs long headline

### Email Subject Line Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| Open Rate | Headline appeal | 20-30%+ |
| Click Rate | Content relevance | 2-5%+ |
| Unsubscribe Rate | Headline/content mismatch | <0.5% |

### Blog Headline Metrics

- Click-through rate from search
- Time on page (does headline match content?)
- Social shares
- Bounce rate

---

## Headline Checklist

Before publishing, verify your headline:

- [ ] Clearly communicates the benefit
- [ ] Matches search intent (for SEO)
- [ ] Creates curiosity without being clickbait
- [ ] Includes target keyword (for SEO content)
- [ ] Appropriate length for platform
  - Blog: 50-60 characters
  - Email: 30-50 characters
  - Social: Platform-specific
- [ ] Uses power words appropriately
- [ ] Delivers on the promise in the content
- [ ] Tested against alternatives
