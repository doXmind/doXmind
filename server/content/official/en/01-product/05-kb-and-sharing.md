# Knowledge Base & Sharing: From Draft to Community

Writing does not happen in a vacuum. Every article, report, or essay draws on sources -- research papers, style guides, reference documents, previous drafts, meeting notes. And once the writing is done, it needs to reach readers. doXmind handles both sides of this equation with its Knowledge Base and Sharing features.

The Knowledge Base gives doXmind AI grounded, source-aware intelligence. Sharing transforms your finished documents into published pieces that readers can access and respond to. Together, they complete the writing lifecycle: from research to draft to publication.

## The Knowledge Base

### What It Is

The Knowledge Base is your personal library within doXmind. It is a collection of documents that you upload and organize for reference. When you chat with doXmind AI, it can search your Knowledge Base, retrieve relevant passages, and use them to inform its suggestions.

Think of it as giving the AI a reading list. Without a Knowledge Base, doXmind AI relies on its general training. With a Knowledge Base, it can cite your specific sources, adhere to your organization's style guide, reference your previous research, and maintain consistency across documents.

### What You Can Store

The Knowledge Base accepts a variety of document types:

- **Research materials** -- Papers, articles, reports, and studies that inform your writing.
- **Style guides** -- Organizational writing standards, brand voice documents, and formatting rules.
- **Reference documents** -- Technical specifications, API documentation, product briefs.
- **Previous drafts** -- Earlier versions of your work that provide context for revisions.
- **Notes and outlines** -- Raw research notes, brainstorming sessions, and structural plans.

### Organizing Your Knowledge Base

Documents in the Knowledge Base can be organized into collections. A collection might correspond to a project, a topic, or a writing genre. For example:

- **"Q4 Report"** -- Contains financial data, team updates, and the previous quarter's report for reference.
- **"Blog Style Guide"** -- Contains your brand voice document, tone guidelines, and examples of published posts.
- **"Research: AI Ethics"** -- Contains academic papers, news articles, and interview transcripts related to your essay topic.

Good organization makes the AI more effective. When doXmind AI searches your Knowledge Base, well-structured collections help it find the most relevant material quickly.

### Using the Knowledge Base in Chat

To leverage your Knowledge Base in a conversation, reference it in your prompts. doXmind AI can search across your uploaded documents to find relevant context.

**Example prompts:**

> "Based on the research in my Knowledge Base, what are the three strongest arguments for adopting microservices?"

> "Search my style guide and tell me whether we use the Oxford comma."

> "Find the revenue figures from last quarter's report in my Knowledge Base and help me write a comparison with this quarter's results."

The AI retrieves relevant passages, cites them in its response, and uses them as the foundation for its suggestions. This means you get answers grounded in your actual sources, not generic information.

### Building an Effective Knowledge Base

The quality of AI assistance scales with the quality of your Knowledge Base. Here are strategies for building one that truly enhances your writing.

**Be selective.** Upload documents that are directly relevant to your current writing projects. A smaller, focused Knowledge Base produces better results than a massive, unfocused one.

**Keep documents current.** If a source has been updated or superseded, replace the old version. Stale information in your Knowledge Base can lead to outdated suggestions.

**Include diverse source types.** Pair data-heavy documents (reports, studies) with narrative ones (articles, essays). This gives the AI both factual grounding and stylistic reference.

**Add your own annotations.** If you highlight key passages or add notes to documents before uploading, the AI benefits from your editorial judgment about what matters most.

## Sharing Your Work

Once your document is polished and ready, doXmind makes it easy to share with others.

### Generating a Share Link

From the editor, open the sharing options for your document. doXmind generates a unique link that you can send to anyone. Recipients can read your beautifully formatted document directly in their browser without creating an account or installing anything.

Share links are designed to present your writing at its best. The formatting, headings, code blocks, images, and other elements render cleanly in a reading-optimized view.

### Sharing Options

doXmind gives you control over how your shared documents are accessed:

- **Public link** -- Anyone with the link can read the document. Ideal for blog posts, published articles, and widely distributed content.
- **Private link** -- Access is restricted. Use this for internal reviews, client deliverables, or draft feedback.

### Use Cases for Sharing

**Getting feedback on drafts.** Share a link with your editor, colleague, or mentor and ask for their thoughts. They see the document exactly as you see it, with all formatting intact.

**Publishing content.** Use doXmind as your writing and publishing platform. Write in the editor, polish with AI assistance, and share the finished piece with your audience.

**Team collaboration.** Share reference documents, style guides, or templates with your team so everyone works from the same source of truth.

**Portfolio building.** Collect your shared links into a portfolio of published writing that demonstrates your expertise.

## The Community Dimension

doXmind is not just a writing tool; it is a writing community. The publishing workflow is designed to help your work reach readers who care about your topics.

### Publishing to the Community

When you publish a document, it becomes discoverable within the doXmind community. Other writers and readers can find, read, and engage with your work.

Publishing is always optional and always under your control. You choose which documents to publish and can unpublish at any time.

### The Feedback Loop

Community publishing creates a natural feedback loop that improves your writing over time.

1. **Write** -- Draft your document in the editor with AI assistance.
2. **Polish** -- Use Quick Edit and the chat panel to refine your work.
3. **Publish** -- Share your document with the community.
4. **Learn** -- Read feedback, observe what resonates with readers, and apply those insights to your next piece.

This cycle accelerates your growth as a writer. Each published piece is a data point about what works and what does not.

### Discovering Other Writers

The community is also a source of inspiration and learning. Browse published documents by other doXmind users to discover new perspectives, writing techniques, and subject-matter expertise.

When you find a piece that impresses you, study its structure. How does the author open? How do they transition between sections? How do they close? These observations feed directly into your own writing practice.

## Knowledge Base and Sharing: Better Together

The Knowledge Base and Sharing features complement each other in a powerful way.

When you add published community content to your Knowledge Base, you create a virtuous cycle. The best writing you discover informs the AI's suggestions for your own writing, which you then polish and publish back to the community.

**A practical example:**

1. You are writing an article about engineering leadership.
2. You add three excellent published articles on the topic to your Knowledge Base.
3. In the chat panel, you ask doXmind AI: "Based on my Knowledge Base sources, what leadership frameworks are most commonly cited?"
4. The AI identifies patterns across your sources and helps you position your own perspective in relation to existing thinking.
5. You write your article, informed but not derivative, and publish it.
6. Someone else discovers your article, adds it to their Knowledge Base, and the cycle continues.

This is writing as a community practice -- each piece building on what came before, each writer contributing to a shared body of knowledge.

## Tips for the Full Workflow

- **Start with the Knowledge Base.** Before you write, upload your research. Give doXmind AI the context it needs from the beginning.
- **Write with AI assistance.** Use autocomplete for momentum, quick edit for refinement, and chat for strategic guidance.
- **Review before sharing.** Use the AI for a final review pass. Ask it to check for clarity, consistency, and completeness.
- **Share strategically.** Not every document needs to be public. Use private links for drafts and public links for finished work.
- **Engage with the community.** Read other writers, provide feedback, and learn from the diversity of styles and perspectives.
