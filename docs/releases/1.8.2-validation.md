## Squirrel update gate

A clean application directory was populated from the public `v1.8.1` update
ZIP at its recorded SHA-256
`69929b3e6e04285150f5dd46319741603243afd889b733a54435df19110b1643`.
The signed `1.8.1` App contacted a loopback Squirrel JSON feed that served the
unchanged Draft `1.8.2` ZIP at SHA-256
`805a5ecda7cb46bc142386e423436743eaecd83f82f04f8790b24b15d57ab3ee`.

Squirrel emitted checking, available, and downloaded events for `1.8.2`.
`quitAndInstall` handed the update to ShipIt, which replaced the installed App
and relaunched it with a live Renderer. The relaunched App reported version
`1.8.2`; its deep signature and stapled ticket remained valid, and its ASAR
matched the candidate at
`7b7320cf7f7b3d9fae2cda1a882fa6f055d99ab6382acb5af8e1fbfeaa2bda02`.

After a clean quit, the updated App was cold-launched through the packaged GUI
suite with fresh isolated user data and passed all 22 checks again. The test
never replaced `/Applications/doXmind.app` and did not open or modify a user
workspace.
